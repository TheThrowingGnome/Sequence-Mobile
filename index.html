    How to play

    1. Play a card, place a chip. Tap a card in your hand, then tap one of the two matching spaces on the board. You can also tap a space directly if you hold its card.
    2. Win with Sequences. A Sequence is five chips of your colour in a row: across, down, or diagonal. With two players or two teams you need two Sequences. With three players or three teams, one wins.
    3. Teams. Teammates share a colour and take turns in between the other teams. You can't see your partner's cards.
    4. Corners are free. The four ★ corners count for everyone.
    5. Two-eyed Jacks (♦ ♣) are wild. Place a chip on any open space.
    6. One-eyed Jacks (♥ ♠) remove. Take any opposing chip off the board, unless it is part of a finished Sequence.
    7. Dead cards. If both spaces for a card are taken, trade it for a new card once per turn, then play.
    8. Sharing. Your second Sequence can reuse one chip from your first.
